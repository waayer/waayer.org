Privacy Policy

What Data We Collect

waayer.org is a privately operated hobby platform hosted in New Zealand. The administrator, waayer, is responsible for the personal information held by these services and can be contacted at admin@waayer.org.

We try to collect as little data as possible. Cloudflare analytics are disabled, and our web server (Caddy) does not collect usage data. Hosted applications may still process account details, IP addresses, approximate location derived from an IP address, device information, and the content you submit. The disclosures below cover that collection.

Hosted Services

The administrator operates the services and can access account information and operational records to provide support, secure accounts, and diagnose faults. The recipients and retention rules below apply alongside the shared rules in the next section.

Authentik: Username, email address, hashed password, login timestamps, and session tokens are used for authentication and access control. Account identity is provided to the services you sign in to. Account details are retained for the lifetime of your account; session tokens are retained until they expire or are revoked.

Jellyfin and Tracearr: Username, playback history, media preferences, device information, IP addresses, and approximate location are used to stream media, maintain watch history, monitor playback, and detect account abuse. Jellyfin activity is processed by Tracearr and is available to the administrator. Account and playback records are retained while your account is active and needed for these purposes; you can request their removal.

Immich: Uploaded photos and videos and their EXIF metadata (which may include GPS location, camera details, and timestamps) are used to host your photo library. Content is available to you and anyone you choose to share it with, and the administrator has server access. Media and metadata are retained until you delete them or your account is removed.

Seerr: Username, linked Jellyfin account, and media request history are used to manage requests. Requests are available to the administrator and passed to the media automation services for fulfilment. Account and request records are retained while needed to manage your account and requests; you can request their removal.

Minecraft: Collects everything that is set to collect by default. Logs are kept after server restarts. Only whitelisted players can join the server.

Find My Device (FMD): Encrypted device location, battery status, and push commands are used to locate and manage your device. Encrypted content is delivered to your authorised devices; the administrator operates the server but cannot read the encrypted payloads. Device records are retained while needed for your registered device, until deleted or the registration is removed. See the FMD privacy policy for service-specific handling.

Anchor (Notes): Documents, content you create, and your username from Authentik are used for note-taking and document storage. Content is available to you, people you share it with, and the administrator with server access. It is retained until you delete it or your account is removed.

Vaultwarden: Client-side encrypted vault data, account email, and IP address are used to store and synchronise your password vault and secure access. Encrypted vault data is delivered to your authorised clients; the administrator can access account metadata but cannot read the encrypted vault contents without your decryption keys. Vault data is retained until you delete it or your account is removed.

ntfy: Topic subscriptions, notification content, and delivery metadata are used to send push notifications. Notifications are delivered to subscribers to the topic, so do not put sensitive information in a public topic. Subscriptions are retained while active; cached notifications and delivery records are retained only while needed for delivery and troubleshooting.

SearXNG: Search queries are processed to aggregate search results and sent to the search providers selected for the request. Queries are not logged or stored locally beyond the request. Those providers process received queries under their own privacy policies; avoid including personal information in searches.

Zipline: Files uploaded by the administrator are served publicly; anyone with a file link can receive the content. Viewer IP addresses and access timestamps are used to deliver files and investigate abuse. Files are retained until removed by the administrator; access records are retained only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Media automation (Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, qBittorrent): Authentik controls access. Search and download history and indexer API interactions are used to acquire and manage media. The administrator and authorised users can access these records; searches and download requests go to the relevant indexers, download providers, and torrent peers. Local history is retained for the lifetime of the service and can be cleared on request.

Homepage and Uptime Kuma: The static website and public status page require no account. Uptime Kuma publishes service availability metrics. Caddy usage logging and Cloudflare analytics are disabled, but Cloudflare still processes connection information as described below. The homepage also publishes aggregate media, request, storage, and Tracearr usage counts.

Shared Purposes, Recipients, and Retention

Information is used to provide the service you choose, authenticate you, deliver content, prevent abuse, and resolve faults. Where GDPR applies, processing needed to provide a requested service is based on performance of that service agreement (Article 6(1)(b)); security and troubleshooting are based on our legitimate interests in keeping the services secure and reliable (Article 6(1)(f)). Account details and content are voluntary, but without the information needed for a feature we cannot provide it.

Service data is stored on privately owned hardware in New Zealand. Cloudflare handles tunnelling and DDoS protection and receives IP addresses and request metadata even with analytics disabled. It may process information outside New Zealand and the EEA. Its recipients, retention criteria, and transfer safeguards are described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. Search and download providers may also process requests outside these regions under their own policies.

If you use Discord to sign in through Authentik or contact the administrator, Discord processes your account and interaction data, and Authentik receives the identity details you authorise. Local identity data follows the Authentik retention rules; Discord's own retention and recipients are covered by Discord's privacy policy.

Personal information is not sold or used for advertising. In addition to the recipients identified above, information may be disclosed where legally required. Application security and diagnostic records, including IP addresses and device metadata, are kept only as long as needed to resolve faults or investigate abuse. Account data is retained for the lifetime of the account and can be removed on request, subject to applicable legal requirements. Functional login cookies and session tokens remain until expiry or revocation. Provider retention is governed by the linked provider policies.

Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, where applicable, you can request access to and correction of your personal information. If a correction is declined, you can ask for a statement of correction to be attached. You can also ask us to remove your account or content. Contact the administrator below; access and correction requests will be decided within 20 working days, subject to any lawful extension. You can complain to the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner.

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability, subject to the conditions and exceptions for each right. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact the administrator below to exercise these rights. Requests will be answered within one month, subject to a permitted extension, and you may complain to your competent data protection supervisory authority.
